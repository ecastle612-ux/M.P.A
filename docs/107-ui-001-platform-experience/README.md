## Purpose

Capture the design principles that made the **Tenant Home** feel friendly, modern, premium, and calm — then apply them as the **platform-wide experience standard** for every portal.

UI-001 is the future **Platform Experience Redesign**: not a single screen refresh, but a consistent way M.P.A. answers:

> **What should I do today?**

…instead of listing modules.
