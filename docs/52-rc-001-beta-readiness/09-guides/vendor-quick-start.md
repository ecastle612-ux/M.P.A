# Vendor Quick Start

**RC-001 · Design Partner beta**

## Important

There is **no Vendor Portal** and vendors are **not** signed-in M.P.A. users.

Vendors participate only through **secure invitation/action links** (email or SMS) generated by M.P.A. Property managers manage vendor company records in the **Vendor Directory**.

## What to expect

1. Your PM creates/assigns a work order to your vendor record.  
2. You receive a secure link (and often a QR) for that work order.  
3. Open the link on your phone — no app install and no password.  
4. Accept or decline, start the job, upload photos, add notes, and mark complete from the link.  
5. Invoice upload (when enabled) also uses the same secure-link surface.

## Internal staff

Property managers and facility technicians continue to assign vendors, review job history, invoices, receipts, and performance inside M.P.A. Operations / Facility workflows.
