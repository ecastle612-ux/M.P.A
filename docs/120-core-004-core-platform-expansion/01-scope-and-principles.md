## Expansion principles

1. **Workflows over features** — every slice delivers an end-to-end operational capability ([07](./07-workflow-requirement.md)).  
2. **Capabilities over chrome** — finish operator outcomes; do not invent layouts.  
3. **Inherit, don’t reinvent** — STD-001 + mandatory standards are default law ([02](./02-ux-inheritance-contract.md)).  
4. **One authorize at a time** — phase/slice-sized delivery with Verify → Certify before the next.  
5. **Entitlement-safe** — AUTH / BILL / capability gates remain authoritative.  
6. **Evidence-first** — unit, integration, a11y, performance, security, screenshots, workflow certification.  
7. **HQ discipline** — Master Admin remains Mission Control + Hybrid C (MAC-002); no new MA launchers.  
8. **Respect CORE-003** — where cross-package order still applies, do not jump the queue without amendment.
