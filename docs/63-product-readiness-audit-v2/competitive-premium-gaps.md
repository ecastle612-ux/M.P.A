# Competitive Premium Gaps — AppFolio · Buildium · Yardi

**Final question:**  
*If this product were competing against AppFolio, Buildium, and Yardi today… what would immediately make it feel less premium?*

**Date:** 2026-08-10  
**Code changes:** None

## Immediate “less premium” signals

1. **Facility Operations nav full of Planned / not-implemented pages** after buying Complete Platform — incumbents rarely sell empty primary nav destinations.  
2. **Resident home advertising Coming soon / Soon** — reads as beta, not production property tech.  
3. **Technician/Vendor portal feels like a stub** next to resident’s polished bottom nav.  
4. **Silent email stub risk** — support saying “we resent the invite” when nothing sent destroys trust faster than any visual bug.  
5. **Flat marketing** — calm brand is good; missing elevation, proof (logos/testimonials), and CTA hierarchy vs incumbent sites.  
6. **Inconsistent components** — raw buttons/tables beside design-system primitives reads as unfinished craft.  
7. **Blank first paint** on many authenticated routes (almost no `loading.tsx`) — incumbents skeleton aggressively.  
8. **Maintenance/ops density on phone** — desktop dual-pane that collapses poorly vs field-ready incumbent mobile.  
9. **“Integration Planned” screening copy in leasing** — honest, but competitors hide roadmap language from daily ops chrome.  
10. **View As living under `/admin/testing/`** — owners expect support tooling, not a lab URL.  
11. **Thin enterprise page** — no credible security/process proof for buyers comparing to Yardi-class procurement.  
12. **Documents CAD/video placeholder** — upload chooser advertising unfinished formats.  
13. **Vendors as a near-empty hub** — nav item without a real directory.  
14. **Dual search metaphors** in the PM shell — cognitive tax vs one command palette.  
15. **Hardcoded hex / Tailwind gray drift** from Canopy — subtle, but designers feel it; craft compounds into “not premium.”  
16. **Comparison tables as plain dots** on marketing — incumbents use richer iconography and savings callouts.  
17. **Demo without product visuals** — text CTAs vs screenshot-led trials.  
18. **Orphan admin reference pages** reachable by URL — softens the “operations console” story.  
19. **Impersonation banner off-brand colors** — necessary visibility, but looks bolted on.  
20. **Overall completeness asymmetry** — PM + commercial strong; FO + tech field weak — competitors feel more evenly deep even when modules differ.

## What already feels premium / differentiated

- Clear **three-product constitution** (Enterprise as sales motion) — sharper than confused tier soups.  
- **Owner Ops slim nav** (only live tools) — the right standard; should spread.  
- Calm **Canopy-forward** marketing typography (Satoshi/Plex) — not Inter-default AI sludge.  
- Honest FO commercial gating (`EARLY ACCESS`) on public pricing — better than lying; must align post-login nav.

## Bottom line

M.P.A. loses the premium comparison **first on unfinished theater inside the product**, **second on mobile field/resident incomplete cards**, **third on craft consistency**, not on the absence of Capital Projects or Marketplace. Fix theater + trust + mobile before new roadmap features.
