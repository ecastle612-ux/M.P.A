# 01 — Vision & Customer Promise

**Parent:** [FAC-OPS-001](./index.md)  
**Status:** Approved

---

## Mission

Facility Operations makes the physical plant of a building portfolio operable, predictable, and auditable — so stewards know what they own, what is due, what failed, and what to do next without living in spreadsheets or disconnected CMMS tools.

---

## Product purpose

Facility Operations is a **first-class M.P.A. subscription** that owns stewardship of the physical property:

- Asset registry and lifecycle  
- Building systems model  
- Preventive maintenance programs  
- Inventory and parts  
- Facility inspections  
- Safety and facility compliance programs  
- Facility operational attention (Mission Control)  
- Operational reporting for facility health  

It does **not** own residential leasing, residents, rent, or unit make-ready turnover. Those remain Property Manager.

---

## Customer promise

> “I can see every critical asset and system, know what preventive and compliance work is due, open corrective work without losing context, and prove to leadership that the facility is under control — in one operating system that already runs my organization.”

Promises that must remain true:

1. **One OS** — FO is not a bolted-on CMMS with a second login identity model.  
2. **Program vs execution** — FO generates and manages programs; Maintenance executes work orders.  
3. **Honest SKU** — Facility-only customers never see PM leasing/rent homes; PM-only customers never see FO modules.  
4. **Master Admin can test everything** — no hidden FO capability.  
5. **Extend, never duplicate** — Mission Control, Documents, Communications, Search, Audit, Assistant are reused.

---

## Target customers

| Segment | Fit |
|---------|-----|
| Commercial / mixed-use facility operators | Primary |
| Multifamily owners running dedicated facility teams | Primary |
| Campus / portfolio facility directors | Primary |
| Property managers selling Complete Platform | Secondary (union SKU) |
| Pure residential PM shops without facility teams | Not FO SKU (use PM Maintenance only) |

---

## Success metrics

| Metric | Intent |
|--------|--------|
| Time-to-first-asset-registry | Site profile → first assets recorded in Guided Setup |
| % critical assets with PM schedule | Preventive coverage |
| PM on-time completion rate | Program effectiveness (via WO completion signals) |
| Open safety / compliance overdue count | Risk posture |
| Mean time from FO attention item → WO accepted | Program→execution handoff |
| Storeroom stockout events on critical parts | Inventory health |
| Master Admin certification Pass rate per slice | Operator readiness |
| Zero duplicate WO homes reported in UX audit | Architecture integrity |

---

## Non-goals (product)

- Replacing specialized engineering CAD / BIM suites  
- Enterprise GL / CapEx accounting ERP (Capital Projects is portfolio ops; finance ERP stays out)  
- Residential lease inspections / move-in/out (Property Manager)  
- Resident maintenance request intake UX (Property Manager Maintenance)  
- Second vendor marketplace
